Privacy Policy

AlphaPiTopUp ("we", "us") is an app that lets you buy Nigerian mobile airtime and data bundles using Pi. This policy explains what information we handle and why. Last updated: October 28, 2026.

What we collect

  • Phone number that you enter, so we can deliver the airtime or data.
  • Order details: the network, the service (airtime or data), the data plan, and the amount.
  • Pi payment details: the payment ID and transaction ID of your Pi payment, and your Pi username as provided by Pi when you sign in.

We do not ask for your Pi passphrase, password, or any financial account details. Never share them with anyone.

How we use it

  • To confirm your Pi payment and deliver the airtime or data to the phone number you entered.
  • To investigate problems with an order and to respond to your messages.

Who we share it with

To complete your order, we send the phone number, network, and plan or amount to our airtime and data supplier (VTpass). Your Pi payment is processed by Pi Network. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Storage

We do not keep a user account database. Order information passes through our server and our hosting provider, which may keep technical logs for a limited time. We keep order records only as long as needed to support you and to meet any legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where we are able to, by emailing us at the address below.

Children

This app is not intended for people under 18.
